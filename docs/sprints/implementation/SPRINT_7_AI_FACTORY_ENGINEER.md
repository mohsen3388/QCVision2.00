# VisionQC 2.0 — Sprint 7: AI Factory Engineer

## Purpose
Sprint 7 adds a safe orchestration layer over Factory Memory, Root Cause Intelligence,
Factory Knowledge Graph, Predictive Quality and Voice AI.

## Delivered
- Factory-wide or case-specific operational briefing
- Operational priority: Routine, Attention, Urgent, Hold and verify
- Executive quality summary
- Predictive-quality summary
- Root-cause hypothesis summary
- Prioritized actions with owner, rationale, verification method and evidence source
- Explicit human decision gates
- Briefing persistence inside Factory Memory case metadata
- Events for briefing generation and escalation recommendation
- Voice AI intent for operational engineer briefings
- Dedicated AI Factory Engineer UI page

## Safety boundary
The service never changes recipes, process settings or equipment state. It recommends
controlled actions and requires authorized QC/process approval.
